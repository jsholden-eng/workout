Equipment
  • Barbell (45 lbs) — plates: 2×45, 2×35, 2×25, 2×10, 2×5
  • EZ Curl Bar (35 lbs) — plates: 2×25, 6×10, 2×5, 2×2.5
  • Dumbbells: 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40 lbs
  • Resistance bands: Light, Medium, Heavy
  • Loop bands: Light → X-Heavy (pull-up assist & activation)
Program Structure
  • Block 1 (Wks 1–4): Foundation — 85–95% intensity, higher reps. Week 4 deload.
  • Block 2 (Wks 5–8): Accumulation — push toward new bests. Week 8 deload.
  • Block 3 (Wks 9–12): Intensification — peak weights, PRs. Week 12 deload.
  • 4 flexible training days per week — no fixed days.
How to Log
  • Enter weight and reps for each set, then tap ✓ to mark it done.
  • Tap ↑ Save after every session. Tap ↓ Load on a new device.
  • If you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add weight next week.
  • On deload weeks, complete the sets but do not push intensity.
Band Notes
  • Resistance bands progress light→medium→heavy across the three blocks.
  • Loop band pull-up assist: heavier = easier. Goal is less assistance each block.
  • DB ceiling at 40 lbs — progress with slower eccentrics or pause reps.
